# 🎬 Netflix Content Intelligence & EDA Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a dataset of 7,700+ Netflix titles integrated with IMDb ratings and metadata. The aim is to analyze content distribution (Movies vs. TV Shows), platform content quality, leading production countries, and genre performance.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Content Distribution:** Approximately **69%** Movies vs. **31%** TV Shows.
- **Content Quality:** TV Shows receive significantly higher viewer ratings on IMDb (average **6.94**) compared to Movies (average **6.21**).
- **Geographic Production:** The United States leads in total content volume (~37%), followed by India (~12.3%) and the United Kingdom.
- **Genre Performance:** While **Dramas** and **Comedies** dominate content quantity, **Documentaries** (7.03) and **Crime TV Shows** (7.08) achieve the highest overall user satisfaction.
